## 🌟 Our Core Commitment: Zero Server Uploads

Unlike legacy PDF utilities (such as iLovePDF or Smallpdf) that upload sensitive files to third-party cloud servers, **AnTPDF is fundamentally architected to preserve your privacy:**

- **Zero Document Data Sent to Servers:** 100% of PDF parsing, rendering, manipulation, and compilation runs entirely locally inside your browser using WebAssembly and HTML5 Canvas.
- **Zero Document Retention:** We do not have servers, databases, or cloud buckets holding your files. When you close the browser tab, your document is erased from local device RAM.
- **Inspect It Yourself:** You can verify this anytime by opening your browser's Developer Tools (`F12` > `Network` tab). You will see **0 bytes** of document payloads leaving your computer.

---

## 📊 Data Collection & Analytics

1. **Personal Information:** We do **not** collect your name, email address, physical address, or phone number to use our tools. No account registration is required.
2. **Cookies & Tracking:** We do **not** use tracking cookies, behavioral ad pixels, or third-party profiling trackers.
3. **Local Storage:** The website may store minimal UI state (such as your preferred dark/light theme) in your browser's `localStorage`. This never contains document content.

---

## 📜 Compliance with Global Privacy Principles

- **GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation):** Because no personal data or documents are collected, stored, or processed on external servers, AnTPDF inherently satisfies data minimization (Article 5) and storage limitation requirements.
- **CCPA / CPRA (California Consumer Privacy Act):** We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with data brokers or advertisers.
